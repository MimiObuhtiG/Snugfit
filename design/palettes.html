Three darker looks for SnugFit

Each phone shows the same Today screen and workout timer in a different dark palette. Pick the one you'd want to open at 7 a.m. and at 10 p.m.

Who uses fitness apps

Roughly 60% of fitness app users are women, and the average user is about 35. SnugFit's lazy-day, at-home angle points to women in their 30s to 50s who are starting out or starting again.

What colors people like

Blue is the most popular color worldwide. Women rate purple much higher than men do, and many people dislike plain brown and yellow. Purples and berry tones suit your audience.

Dark screens

About half of health and fitness apps open in dark mode, and most people keep their phones dark at night. Training apps tend to use a dark base with one bright accent. Calm apps lean warmer and softer.

A · Night Bloom My pick

Your current pink and plum, set on a deep aubergine. It keeps the look you like, feels warm instead of techy, and leans into the purple tones your audience favors.

B · Deep Lagoon

Dark teal with a coral accent and warm sand highlights. Calm and fresh, closer to wellness apps, and less tied to pink if you want SnugFit to feel more neutral.

C · Ink & Citrus

Near-black with a lime accent and violet hero. Sporty and energetic, like a training app. It stands out, but it's the furthest from SnugFit's cozy, lazy-day personality.

How I'd ship it: whichever you pick becomes the dark theme, and the app follows your phone's light or dark setting automatically, with a manual switch under Me. The current light pink look stays as the light theme unless you'd like that refreshed too.
